# Persona Test

Aplikasi Tes Kepribadian MBTI Online & Panduan Potensi Diri dalam Mencapai Karir Terbaik\* — an online MBTI-style personality test and a guide to your strengths for a better career. Persona Test is an online personality test and a guide to finding a career that fits who you are.

**Live app:** [test.persona.my.id](https://test.persona.my.id)

_Aplikasi Tes Kepribadian MBTI Online & Panduan Potensi Diri dalam Mencapai Karir Terbaik_ — an online MBTI-style personality test and a guide to your strengths for a better career.

## What you can do

1. **Take the test.** Answer a series of questions. You do not need an account to start.
2. **See your result.** You get a personality type, how strong each trait is, and a short picture of that type. You can share the result.
3. **Create an account.** Signing up saves your result so you can come back later.
4. **Read and learn.** Articles explain personality, the four color groups, and each of the 16 types. After you sign in, you also get guides written for your own type.

## The personality types

People are grouped into four colors, and each color has four types:

| Color     | Types                                                                                  |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gold**  | The Supervisor (ESTJ), The Inspector (ISTJ), The Provider (ESFJ), The Protector (ISFJ) |
| **Red**   | The Promoter (ESTP), The Crafter (ISTP), The Performer (ESFP), The Composer (ISFP)     |
| **Blue**  | The Commander (ENTJ), The Mastermind (INTJ), The Inventor (ENTP), The Thinker (INTP)   |
| **Green** | The Mentor (ENFJ), The Counselor (INFJ), The Champion (ENFP), The Dreamer (INFP)       |

## Guides for your type

Once you have an account, your menu includes articles matched to your result:

- Natural strengths
- An ideal workplace
- Careers that may suit you
- The kind of boss you work best with
- Challenges that often come with your personality

Reading articles and finishing the test also add points, so you can track how far you have gone.
